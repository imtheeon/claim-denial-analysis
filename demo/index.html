Where a hospital's $74.1M in denied claims comes from

120,000 claims, January 2021 to June 2024. Payer, specialty and diagnosis barely change the denial rate. Documentation quality does. Move the slider to see what fixing it could be worth.

Documentation score is the whole story

Share of claims denied, by the documentation completeness score recorded with each claim (auth-required claims). Every claim scoring under 0.5 was denied; none at 0.9 or above was. That is cleaner than real payers behave, which is why the dollar estimate below is an upper bound.

What would a documentation check be worth?

Hold every claim scoring under 0.7 for review before it is filed. Choose how many of those held claims get fixed in time. The fixed ones are assumed to deny at the same 1.7% as claims that scored 0.7 or higher.
